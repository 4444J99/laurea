LAVREA exists to make exceptional, externally useful distinctions **provable and durable**.

This ledger separates three things that should never again be conflated:

1. **measured facts** — directly reproducible from GitHub or another named source;
2. **derived distinctions** — conclusions that follow from a stated denominator and method;
3. **externally attributed distinctions** — claims first supplied by a recruiter, award body, client, maintainer, or other third party, retained with provenance while their original source artifact is recovered.

## Publication rule

Be aggressive about **discovering** distinctions and conservative about **mislabeling** them.

A claim may be prominent when its provenance is explicit. A measured fact must remain a measured fact; an attributed recruiter finding must remain attributed until its original calculation is recovered; a percentile becomes a LAVREA-computed percentile only when the comparison population and code are versioned and reproducible.

This prevents both failure modes: unsupported boasting **and** the equally damaging erasure of real achievements merely because a later reconstruction used a different method.
