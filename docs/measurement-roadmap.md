A success in this pass is an additional factual quantity or a stronger evidence
status, not necessarily a larger number. Implementation claims stay scoped to the
inspected sources. No private IP is copied into the public evidence repository.

## Then: recover non-GitHub scale from primary records

**Teaching:** unique institution/term/course/section tuples; enrollment seats and
unique learners as distinct counts; scheduled versus delivered contact hours;
assignment/rubric versions and anonymized assessed-work counts. Course letters
are leads, not the underlying ledger. Publish aggregates only, never student data.

**Creative reception:** credited works and release IDs; screenings, selections,
platform view/play snapshots and repeat use where available. Attribute specific
roles. Do not label view totals unique viewers or sum overlapping platform reports
without identifying the overlap.

**Client and institutional outcomes:** a delivered artifact, baseline, observation
period and result. Count campaign reach, conversions, time saved or funds supported
only with attributable evidence and publication permission. Distinguish a team's
result from the individual's contribution; do not invent causal credit.

## Repeated discovery, once explicitly scheduled

A useful scheduled run must collect new evidence, compute it, compare with the
accepted record, produce a tested update, and report the artifact and change.
A no-change run should not manufacture a new achievement. Failures retain the last
valid dated evidence rather than erase it or silently relabel it current.

No new repository is needed. Do not multiply correlated rankings, manufacture a
single exceptionalism score, or convert quantity into quality without the appropriate
comparison. Keep discovering distinct measurable dimensions of the whole practice.
